# 🌿 National Hackathon on Human-Wildlife Coexistence
## 🏛 Ministry of Environment, Forest & Climate Change (MoEFCC) + Wildlife Institute of India (WII)

> A prestigious national government hackathon aimed at developing technology-driven AI, sensor networks, and early warning solutions for human-wildlife conflict mitigation and ecological conservation.

---

### 📅 Event Details

- **Event:** National Hackathon on Human-Wildlife Coexistence  
- **Organizers:** Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (Govt. of India) & Wildlife Institute of India (WII)  
- **Role:** National Hackathon Participant & Core Developer  

---

### 💡 Technical Focus

- Designed early-warning telemetry and sensor-alert frameworks utilizing computer vision and edge IoT concepts.
- Presented technical workflows addressing real-time detection, intrusion alerts, and geo-tagged incident logging.

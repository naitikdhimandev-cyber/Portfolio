# Certified Ethical Hacker (CEH)

**Issuing Organization:** EC-Council (International Council of E-Commerce Consultants)  
**Credential Status:** Verified Industry Certification  
**Domain:** Penetration Testing, Cyber Defense, Threat Intelligence & Ethical Hacking  

---

## Overview

The Certified Ethical Hacker (CEH) is the world's most recognized vendor-neutral ethical hacking and cybersecurity certification. It validates comprehensive practical and theoretical competencies across 20 security domains, simulating real-world adversary tactics, techniques, and procedures (TTPs).

---

## Core Competencies & Validated Domains

- **Footprinting & Reconnaissance:** OSINT gathering, DNS enumeration, active/passive network scanning.
- **Vulnerability Analysis:** Network vulnerability assessment, automated vulnerability scanning, risk scoring (CVSS).
- **System Hacking:** Password cracking techniques, privilege escalation, steganography, clearing audit logs.
- **Malware Threats:** Trojan analysis, ransomware defense mechanics, virus/worm payloads, dynamic behavioral analysis.
- **Sniffing & Network Attacks:** ARP poisoning, MAC flooding, DNS spoofing, packet inspection using Wireshark.
- **Social Engineering:** Phishing vectors, credential harvesting mechanics, identity impersonation countermeasures.
- **Web Application Security:** OWASP Top 10 auditing, SQL Injection (SQLi), Cross-Site Scripting (XSS), CSRF, and broken access control testing.
- **Wireless & IoT Security:** WPA/WPA2/WPA3 auditing, Bluetooth vulnerability assessments, IoT protocol security.
- **Cloud & Cryptography:** Public key infrastructure (PKI), symmetric/asymmetric cipher suites, cloud attack vectors, container security.

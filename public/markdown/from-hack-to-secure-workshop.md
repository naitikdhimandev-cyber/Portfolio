---

## 🧠 Comprehensive Curriculum & Hands-On Modules

### 1. 🛡️ Cybersecurity & Threat Modeling
- **CIA Triad in Practice**: Confidentiality, integrity, and availability trade-offs in modern distributed and IoT networks.
- **Attack Surface Analysis**: Mapping entry points, identifying weak nodes, privilege escalation vectors, and misconfigurations.

### 2. 🎣 Phishing & Social Engineering Vectors
- **Anatomy of Deception**: Examining spear-phishing payloads, malicious links, and deceptive email headers.
- **URL Manipulation & Heuristics**: Recognizing obfuscated domains, homograph attacks, and suspicious web triggers.

### 3. 🔐 Cryptography & Data Integrity
- **Ciphers & Hashing Algorithms**: Practical mechanics of symmetric/asymmetric encryption, hashing (SHA-256), and HMACs.
- **Data Protection in Transit**: Securing endpoints and verifying cryptographic message integrity against tampering.

### 4. 🌐 Secure Communication Protocols
- Implementing and demonstrating encrypted transport tunnels, TLS handshakes, and hardened socket communication.

### 5. 💥 Controlled Attack Demonstrations (Isolated Lab)
- **Denial of Service (DoS)**: Simulating resource-exhaustion, packet flooding, and rate-limiting failure in safe, sandboxed test environments.
- **Defensive Mitigation**: Implementing rate-limiting, firewall rule sets, and anomaly drop filters.

### 6. 🕵️ Man-in-the-Middle (MITM) & Traffic Interception
- Demonstrating ARP spoofing and unencrypted packet inspection.
- The critical role of continuous network monitoring, certificate pinning, and mutual authentication.

## 🛠️ Live Hardware & IoT Security Showcase

One of the marquee segments of the workshop was a live demonstration of custom **IoT-based cybersecurity monitoring systems and hardware tools** built by Naitik:
- **Hardware-based Network Telemetry**: Real-time packet inspection and abnormal activity logging on embedded microcontrollers.
- **Suspicious Activity & Drift Detection**: Automated alerts when unauthorized network configuration changes or unexpected payload signatures occur.
- **Practical Engineering**: Proving how abstract security theories translate into tangible, physical engineering defenses.

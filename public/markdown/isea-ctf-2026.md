# 🚩 ISEA ISAP CTF 2026 — IIT Madras
## 🏛 Indian Institute of Technology Madras | National Cyber Security CTF

> An elite national-level Capture The Flag (CTF) competition organized under the Information Security Education and Awareness (ISEA) project by the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) at IIT Madras.

---

### 📅 Event Details

- **Event:** ISEA ISAP Capture The Flag (CTF) 2026  
- **Organizer:** IIT Madras & ISEA (MeitY, Govt. of India)  
- **Role:** CTF Competitor  
- **Format:** Jeopardy-Style Security CTF  

---

### 🧠 Challenge Domains

- 🌐 **Web Security:** Exploitation of broken access control, server-side request forgery (SSRF), and injection flaws.
- 🔐 **Applied Cryptography:** Breaking flawed cipher implementations, key recovery, and hash collision analysis.
- 🧩 **Digital Forensics:** Memory dump triage, pcap network traffic analysis, and filesystem artifact recovery.
- 🖥 **Reverse Engineering:** Binary disassembly, control flow analysis, and decoding obfuscated routines.

---

### 🎯 Key Highlights

- Competed against premier cybersecurity teams from top engineering institutes across India.
- Applied rigorous offensive security methodologies and rapid exploit automation scripts.
